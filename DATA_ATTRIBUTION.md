# Data attribution and responsible use

This project uses local ABIDE I phenotypic data obtained through the Autism Brain
Imaging Data Exchange (ABIDE) and the International Neuroimaging Data-sharing
Initiative (INDI). Access ABIDE I through the [ABIDE data-access
page](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide/abide_I.html); users must
follow the applicable registration and access requirements.

ABIDE I is described by INDI as available under a [Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
This repository's public dashboard publishes only aggregate, de-identified
outputs. Raw files, participant records, subject identifiers, and
participant-level derived artifacts are intentionally excluded.

This is an educational portfolio project, not a diagnostic or clinical tool.
ABIDE and INDI do not endorse this project. This notice is attribution and
responsible-use information, not legal advice or a claim of legal compliance.
